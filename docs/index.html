Course Philosophy & Applied AI

This course transitions students from digital consumers to computational builders. Designed specifically alongside Math 1 Algebra standards, students use modern tools without getting bogged down by high-level math theory.


Our AI Approach: "Use, Don't Build (Yet)." Rather than writing complex machine learning models, students leverage generative AI as an everyday coding co-pilot, learn where models physically execute on hardware GPUs, evaluate AI safety/ethics, and train lightweight visual tools like Google Teachable Machine.
